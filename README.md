# HoverTime

**Stay afloat with your timekeeping sidekick.**

HoverTime is a free classroom and workshop timer that floats over your slides. It has ten themed vibes (from calm to game show to a cozy rainy window), built-in music, and partner-pairing prompts that keep a room moving.

**▶ Use it: [coachkarle.github.io/hovertime](https://coachkarle.github.io/hovertime/)**. Nothing to install, and no account needed.

## Highlights

- **Float over your slides:** pops the timer into a small always-on-top window, even over full-screen Google Slides or PowerPoint. Minimize it to a tiny icon, and dock it back anytime. With music on, Chrome can float it automatically when you switch to your slides.
- **Card:** directions that stay on screen with the timer, in Float or full screen. Pick a ready-made protocol (Most Important Point, 3-2-1, What? So What? Now What?, and more) and its time loads too. Click a line to highlight it (N moves to the next), double-click or Edit to change it, or jot a quick sticky note. **Break** shows the clock time everyone should be back. Minimize the card or the timer when you need the room.
- **Your Float (wide screens):** a panel beside the timer to choose what shows (Timer, Card, Pair up). The card sits beside the timer, and Float and Full screen show it just like that.
- **Make it bigger:** drag the grip under the timer down to enlarge the page, if you keep it up instead of floating.
- **More music:** each vibe keeps its own songs as buttons, and a small ⋯ at the end of them plays any other vibe's songs.
- **Ten vibes:** Basic, Sand Timer, Wedge, 8-bit, Synthwave, Sunrise, Grow, Game Show, Cozy Rain, and Sci-Fi. Each has its own look, original music, and sound cues.
- **Pair up, Rotation, Full screen:** random partner prompts, equal turns with a chime at each switch, and a full-screen view with the timer beside big directions or your card. Drag the divider to size the two sides.
- **Make it yours:** color presets (including common school colors), fonts, and palettes.
- **Accessible:** text meets WCAG AA contrast, and animations respect "reduce motion".

## Keyboard

| Key | Action |
| --- | --- |
| Space | Start / pause |
| ↑ / ↓ | ±1 minute |
| 1–9 (0 = 10) | Set minutes |
| R | Reset |
| M | Music on / off |
| G | Pair up prompt |
| C | Card on / off |
| N | Highlight the next line |
| F | Full screen |

## Notes

- Float uses Chrome's Document Picture-in-Picture, so it needs desktop Chrome or Edge 116 or newer. Everything else works in any modern browser.
- It's a single self-contained HTML file. All music and sounds are synthesized live in the browser.
- Your settings and cards are saved only in your own browser. The page loads its fonts from Google Fonts and counts anonymous visits with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com) (no cookies, no personal data). Nothing else leaves your computer.
