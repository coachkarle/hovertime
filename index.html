HoverTimeStay afloat with your timekeeping sidekick

Quick guide

  1. Pick a vibeEach vibe has its own look, music, and sounds.
  2. Float over your slidesA small always-on-top window, even over full-screen slides. With music on, it can float by itself when you switch away (Chrome asks once). – shrinks it to an icon; Dock brings it back.
  3. The ⋯ menuPair up: random partner prompts (shuffle for another). Rotation: equal turns with a chime. Facilitate: full screen with room for instructions. Full screen: just the timer, big (turn your phone sideways).
  4. Set the timeTap or click the digits and type 5, 2:30, or 90s. Or press 1–9.
  5. Make it yours⚙ has common school colors, fonts, and palettes.
